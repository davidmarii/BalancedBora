BalancedBora Privacy Statement

Version 1.0 — Effective 28 September 2026

BalancedBora ("we", "us") is a WhatsApp-based ration formulation service for pig and poultry farmers in Kenya. This statement explains what personal information we collect when you use the service, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

1. Who we are

BalancedBora is operated by David Mwangi. For any privacy question, email david@example.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number. Required to send your ration back to you. We cannot provide the service without it.
  • Your animal and feed selections. The pig or chicken type you choose and the feeds you indicate you have available. Used to compute your least-cost ration.
  • Photos you send. If you send a photo of a feed bag, we analyse it to identify the feed. We do not retain the image after processing.
  • Consent record. The date and time you agreed to this statement, and a copy of the consent text you saw. Retained for legal compliance.

We do not collect your name, national ID, physical location, email address, or any payment information.

3. How we use your information

  • To calculate and send your least-cost ration
  • To respond to your messages and remember your session
  • To comply with WhatsApp Business policy and Kenyan data law
  • In anonymised, aggregated form, to improve our feed composition data

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers, marketers, or any third party except as required to operate the service (see Section 4).

4. Who we share data with

  • Twilio — the platform that connects WhatsApp to our servers. Twilio processes messages on our behalf.
  • Google (Gemini API) — used only to interpret natural-language messages that our local parser cannot handle. Message text is sent to Google for parsing and is not stored by us after the reply is generated.
  • Render — our hosting provider. Your session data lives on their servers in encrypted form.

We never share your phone number with these providers for marketing.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Session data (current conversation): deleted after 24 hours of inactivity.
  • Consent record: retained as long as you use the service, plus 12 months, for legal compliance.
  • Photos: not retained. Deleted immediately after analysis.
  • Suppression list (if you send STOP): retained indefinitely so we do not accidentally message you again.

6. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Object to processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (odpc.go.ke)

7. How to unsubscribe

Send the word STOP at any time on WhatsApp. You will be removed from our active users immediately. Send START to resume.

8. To request deletion of your data

Email balancedbora@gmail.com with your WhatsApp number and the word "DELETE". We will remove your consent record, session data, and any other personal information within 30 days, and confirm by reply.

9. Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. When we do, the version number at the top of this page will change, and we will ask you to re-consent on your next message.
